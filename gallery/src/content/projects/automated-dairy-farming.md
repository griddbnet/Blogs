## Inspiration 
Seeing small dairy farmers struggle with manual work, inconsistent feeding, and late health detection inspired us to create an affordable AI + IoT system that makes dairy farming easier, smarter, and more productive.

## What it does 

The system automates feeding, watering, cleaning, temperature control, fogging, health monitoring, heat detection, calving alerts, and milk quality analysis—all in real time through a mobile dashboard.

## How we built it 
We used ESP32, sensors, actuators, LoRa/Bluetooth modules, and AI models for behaviour analysis. Data flows to a cloud dashboard for alerts and predictions. Each feature is modular so farmers can adopt it easily.

## Challenges
we ran into Ensuring accurate health detection, creating stable communication, calibrating sensors, designing affordable hardware, and managing power for continuous operation in rural conditions.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of 
Building a working prototype that automates major dairy tasks, detecting health and heat patterns early, achieving low-cost hardware design, and creating a system scalable for any size farm.

## What we learned 
Sensor integration, embedded system design, AI-based anomaly detection, IoT communication, hardware reliability, user-centric design, and how to solve real agricultural problems with tech.

## What's next for Automated Dairy Farming 
Add milk adulteration detection, AI diet recommendation, full mobile app, solar-powered units, farm-to-consumer milk traceability, and deployment in real dairy farms for testing and scaling
